PROPOSAL-NORM (phase 1, every docs/issue-<n>/proposals/*.md): state scope/change description, a named risk (a concrete failure mode, never "risks exist"), a rollback/back-out path, cite at least one source (URL or repo path) for every adopted-methodology claim, and state `change_type` as one of the six Keep a Changelog categories (Added/Changed/Deprecated/Removed/Fixed/Security — roles/specs/release-engineering.spec.json, issue-44). Do not write "risk: TBD" or a rollback section that says "revert if needed" without naming the actual revert mechanism. proposal-fields-gate.sh refuses a Write/Edit/MultiEdit to a proposal document missing any of these five, or naming a `change_type` outside the six categories; the refusal names the specific missing section — fix that section and retry, do not restate prose that was already there under a different heading to satisfy the gate's keyword match.
